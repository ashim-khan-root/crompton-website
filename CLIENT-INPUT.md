## 4. Photography — the single biggest gap

The site currently has **25 photo frames, 6 certificate frames and 6 client-logo frames —
all empty**. A manpower supplier is judged on its people, housing, transport and safety
record; a buyer cannot shortlist what they cannot see.

Full brief with framing notes: **`SHOOT-LIST.md`** in the project folder. Summary:

| Set | What | Count | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Hero: a crew at work on a Qatar site, wide, quiet left third | 1 | 🔴 |
| B | Workforce: engineer, mixed crew, trades, general workers, supervisors | 4–6 | 🟠 |
| C | Accommodation: exterior, interior/common area, dining | 2–3 | 🟠 |
| D | Transportation: fleet, workers boarding | 2–3 | 🟠 |
| E | Training: induction, practical trade training | 2–3 | 🟠 |
| F | HSE: toolbox talk, correct PPE, safety board | 2–3 | 🟠 |
| G | Projects staffed (only with the main contractor's permission) | 3–5 | ⚪ |
| H | Office / recruitment team | 2–3 | ⚪ |
| I | Leadership headshots + names and titles | 2–4 | ⚪ |

**Specs:** JPEG q80+, sRGB, long edge **3000px** for hero, 2000px for grid; landscape unless
noted. Naming: `section-subject-01.jpg`.

**Rules:** real photography only (no stock, no AI); written consent from anyone
identifiable; no safety violations visible (missing PPE will undermine the HSE page);
Cromton must own the images or hold an indefinite commercial licence.
