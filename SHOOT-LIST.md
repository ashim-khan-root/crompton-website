# Cromton Group — Photography & Brand Asset Brief

**For:** Cromton Group (client) · **Prepared by:** website team · **Date:** 2026-10-17

This brief is what the website is waiting on. The site is built and works; it cannot look
credible until it shows the operation. A manpower-supply company is judged on its people,
housing, transport and safety record — a buyer cannot shortlist what they cannot see.

Every image below lands in a specific place on the site. Where a slot exists, the site shows a
labelled placeholder with the exact dimensions, so nothing needs re-laying-out when the photo
arrives.

---

## 1. Ground rules

1. **Real photography first.** No stock images, nothing pulled from the internet. AI-generated
   imagery is approved by the client (2026-10-21) **only** for staff-free operational and
   atmosphere scenes that make no factual claim — brief and prompts in `AI-IMAGE-BRIEF.md`.
   People, certificates, client logos, and anything captioned as a specific project or employee
   must be real.
2. **People give consent.** Anyone identifiable must have agreed to appear on the company
   website. Where a client's site or equipment shows, confirm that client permits publication.
3. **No safety violations on camera.** No missing PPE, no one standing under a load, no
   unguarded machinery. An HSE-conscious buyer will spot it, and it undermines the safety claim
   the whole section exists to make.
4. **Rights.** Cromton Group owns the images outright, or holds a licence that covers commercial
   website use indefinitely.
5. **If a subject doesn't exist yet, say so.** A labelled placeholder is better than a
   substitute photo of something else.

## 2. Delivery specification

| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Format | JPEG (quality 80+) for photos; PNG for logos with transparency; PDF or high-res JPG for certificate scans |
| Colour | sRGB |
| Long edge | **3000 px minimum** for full-width/hero images; 2000 px for grid images |
| Orientation | Landscape unless stated (a few portraits are called out) |
| Processing | No heavy filters, no fake colour grading, no added text or watermarks |
| How to send | One folder per section (see naming below), ideally via a shared drive link |

**Naming convention:** `section-subject-01.jpg`
Example: `hero-crew-site-01.jpg`, `accommodation-camp-01.jpg`, `hse-briefing-01.jpg`
